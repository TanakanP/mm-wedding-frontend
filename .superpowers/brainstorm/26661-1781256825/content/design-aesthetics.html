Dominant deep emerald + gold for luxury and nature. Blush and petal for romance and color. Cream base with moss accents. Gradients, soft overlays, and subtle botanical textures (noise + leaf patterns) will create atmosphere and depth. No timid pastels — this will feel lush and memorable.

Typography (Distinctive & Characterful)

M & M
We are getting married
December 5, 2026 • The Garden Hiroen
In a hidden rose garden café, surrounded by climbing vines and the scent of jasmine, we talked for hours as golden light filtered through the leaves.

Headings & display: Playfair Display (already in use — elegant, romantic, timeless). Body & UI: Crimson Pro or similar warm, distinctive serif/semi-serif (avoids generic sans like Inter). Script accents possible with a light cursive for " & " or small flourishes. Generous line-height, refined tracking on headings.

Motion & Interaction Principles

  • Blooming reveals: scale + opacity + gentle rotate on scroll/click (feels like flowers opening)
  • Petals & particles: subtle canvas or framer-driven falling petals in hero; burst on key actions (RSVP success, story bloom)
  • Hover & micro: soft lift, gold glow, tiny petal scatter on cards/buttons
  • Staggered & orchestrated: one beautiful page-load sequence, then scroll-triggered delights. Not cluttered — every motion has purpose.
