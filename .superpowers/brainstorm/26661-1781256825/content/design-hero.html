Key Gimmicks in Hero:

  • Falling Petals: Canvas or lightweight Framer Motion particles (rose, jasmine, wisteria petals) that drift down gently. They respond subtly to mouse movement (gentle wind effect). Low performance cost, beautiful on scroll too.
  • Blooming Countdown: Large elegant timer (months/days/hours/minutes). Numbers "bloom" in with scale + opacity + slight rotate when they change. Color shifts softly from cream to gold as the date approaches. Clicking it can trigger a small petal burst.
  • Entry Gesture: Smooth scroll prompt with a delicate line or vine that animates. The whole hero has a soft "breathing" background gradient to feel alive.

Transition & Overall Page Entry

After hero, a very gentle "enter the garden" moment: the first Our Story section fades up with a slight upward bloom animation. Backgrounds throughout use layered textures (soft leaf patterns, subtle noise, cream-to-moss gradients) so the whole site feels like walking deeper into the garden.

Sticky Garden Navigation

Subtle fixed nav (appears after scroll past hero):

  • Small "M & M" logo (Playfair, gold on hover)
  • Links: Our Story • The Garden Path • Plant Your Wishes • Garden Whispers (FAQ)
  • Flower icons instead of bullets or underlines
  • RSVP button that "blooms" on hover (opens the modal)

Very light — doesn't fight the content, just helps guests navigate the immersive experience.
