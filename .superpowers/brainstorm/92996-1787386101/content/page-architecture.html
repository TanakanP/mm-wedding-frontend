Why this sequence

Emotion first, logistics second

  1. Welcome guests with a strong image and recognizable M & M tag.
  2. Answer the date immediately.
  3. Build emotional connection through the story.
  4. Introduce the event schedule, then give the eye a photographic rest.
  5. Deliver venue and RSVP actions after guests are invested.
  6. Finish with practical answers and personal wishes.

Motion rule: one crafted hero transition, then gentle image reveals. No section should make guests wait for information.
